WHAT IS SUBSONUS

SUBSONUS is a digital archive for Indonesia’s underground scene — with a focus on Jamaican sounds and their derivatives. Ska, Rocksteady, Reggae, Dancehall, Dub, Jungle, and everything that came out of that island.

02
WHY

This scene exists. It’s been around for a long time. But the documentation is scattered — in forgotten WhatsApp groups, on broken hard drives, in photos that were never uploaded. SUBSONUS is here to bring it all together into one proper place.

03
WHAT WE ARCHIVE
▶
MIXTAPES

Archive of mixes from underground sessions — exploring Jamaican sound, from ska and rocksteady to reggae, dub, and beyond.

◉
GIG PHOTOS

Visual documentation of past gigs — moments not meant to be forgotten.

04
LOOKING FORWARD

This is just the beginning. SUBSONUS will continue to grow — more archives, more documentation, more proper. All for the scene that has already given so much.
